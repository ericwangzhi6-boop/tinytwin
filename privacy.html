Privacy Policy

Tiny Twin for Mac and Windows · Last updated November 22, 2026

Tiny Twin is a desktop companion app. It was built to respect your privacy, and it does not collect any data about you.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or share personal information.
  • It does not record what you type, what is on your screen, or which apps or websites you use.
  • It does not send anything about you over the internet. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • The free trial and the one-time unlock are handled by Apple through the App Store on Mac, and by Microsoft through the Microsoft Store on Windows. The app only learns whether your trial is active and whether you have unlocked it. It never sees your payment details.

The one thing it checks

To know when you have stepped away (so your twin can fall asleep), the app asks your computer (macOS or Windows) how many seconds have passed since the last keyboard or mouse input. This is a single number. The app does not see which keys you pressed or where you clicked, and this number never leaves your computer.

Data stored on your computer

Your outfit choices, settings, window position and your twin's mood and energy are saved locally on your computer so they are still there next time. They are not sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

The app does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
