Tiny Twin

A tiny pixel version of you that lives in the corner of your screen, on Mac and Windows. It studies, snacks, naps and daydreams, and gently reminds you to take breaks.

Help & FAQ

Where is the app after I open it?

Your twin appears in the bottom-right corner of your screen. The app lives in the menu bar (look for the smiley face), so there is no Dock icon. Click the smiley face for Dress Up & Settings, size, Always on Top, Open at Login and Quit.

How do I move or resize it?

Drag the twin anywhere. Choose Small, Medium or Large from the menu bar icon or by right-clicking the twin.

Why is my twin asleep?

When you have not touched your keyboard or mouse for a while (5 minutes by default), your twin falls asleep at the desk. Click it to wake it up. You can change or turn off this timer in Settings.

How do break reminders work?

After a long stretch at the computer (45 minutes by default), your twin stands up, stretches and drinks water as a reminder for you to do the same. Choose 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or turn it off in Settings.

Does it read what I type?

No. It only checks how many seconds have passed since your last keyboard or mouse input. It sends nothing about you anywhere and collects no data. See the Privacy Policy.
